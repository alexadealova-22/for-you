something I've been meaning to say, badly

What if you accepted my love?

I've rewritten this a few times already. I still don't really know how to say it right, so I'll just say it plainly. Somewhere between all the dumb jokes and you keeping up with mine, I started feeling something I didn't expect. So, what if you accepted my love? I don't know what would change. Maybe nothing. Maybe everything.

I'm sorry if I'm not a good writer. I promise I did my best writing all of this. :>

I The Question

I won't suddenly become a different person. I'll still overthink sometimes. I'll still have days where I'm just tired or quiet. But one thing I won't let happen, you won't ever have to guess where my heart is.

We both know what it's like to feel unsure. Loving someone and still wondering if part of them is somewhere else. Waiting for reassurance that doesn't come. Trying to convince yourself you're enough.

I don't want that for you. Especially not you.

If one day you asked me, "am I really the only one?", I hope you wouldn't need me to answer.

I hope you'd already know, just from how I've been.

II Peace

I don't want you to keep overthinking. Checking if something changed. Reading too much into a few hours of silence, when it probably just meant someone fell asleep. You've had enough of that.

So have I, honestly.

I just want to give you peace. The kind where you can sleep without wondering who you're being compared to.

"It's okay… I'm safe here."

That peace needs somewhere to live. I hope it could live in me. If I ever became your home, I'd take care of that feeling. Home shouldn't feel temporary. It shouldn't make you nervous every time the door opens.

Somewhere you don't have to pretend you're okay. That's who I want to be for you.

III The Ordinary

I think love is in the small, ordinary moments. Like the time I said something dumb just to mess around, "dasar freaky aping", and you didn't even blink, you just fired back "freakytiw" right away. I wasn't expecting that. I still think about it.

That's the you I fell for. Not some perfect version. Just you, being a little silly, same as me.

Or the time you randomly sent me a photo of yourself, no warning at all, and I just froze for a good minute before I could type anything back.

I never told you that. I'm telling you now.

Or the time you just said "aku mau ke luar dulu", the correct way, not "keluar" like almost everyone types it without thinking. You didn't even do it on purpose. I don't think I've ever noticed anyone get that right before.

And for some reason, that small thing just hit me. Like, damn, this girl actually gets that.

And I want every version of you. The tired one. The one who overthinks a text for ten minutes. The one who has it all together, and the one who clearly doesn't.

All of them deserve the same love, not just the easy one.

IV Staying

If life ever got heavy, I wouldn't disappear. Maybe I'd need some quiet, or some time alone. But that wouldn't be a reason to stop choosing you. Some people only stay when things are easy. I want to be different.

Trust isn't built by saying "you can trust me." It's built on normal days. Regular conversations.

On small promises that get kept, even when no one's watching to check.

I hope one day you stop bracing for something bad to happen. Not because you forget the past, but because I keep showing up the same way, again and again.

Until there's just no reason left to be afraid.

V The Truth

I'd never ask you to forget what you've been through. Those things shaped you, and some shaped me too. But I hope they don't stop us from believing in something good.

Maybe we'd still argue sometimes. We're only human. But I hope we'd keep talking, keep listening, and never let being right matter more than understanding each other.

The truth is, I don't want you to love me while wondering if I'm about to leave.

I want you to feel sure of me, so sure that the question just stops coming up.

So, what if you accepted my love? I'd spend every day making sure you feel at ease. Not to prove anything. Just because that's what love should feel like.

One day, I hope you'll look back and realize, you never had to compete with anyone from my past. You never had to earn your place.

It was already yours, from the moment I decided to choose you, and I'll always try to choose you.

Alek, the person who calls you "alay" all the time
